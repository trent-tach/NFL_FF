## Design Specificity Verdict

**Category-interchangeable.** A competent SaaS dashboard with football data poured in. Swap player names for company names and `/redraft` is an admin CRM table.

Missing vs competitive set: team color as structural device; **bye weeks** (most-checked field in fantasy, absent everywhere); injury designations; **positional tier breaks** (FantasyPros' defining signature); ADP-vs-rank delta; "last updated" stamp (staleness is the #1 trust question in this category).

The one football-native artifact is the `/games` card — and `GameCard.tsx` picks accent from `ACCENTS[index % 8]`, commented "purely for visual variety (not tied to any data)." Opposite of what a sports site does with color. `--color-primary: #1d4ed8` is stock Tailwind blue-700; `styles/index.css` has four tokens total.

**Deterministic scan — non-diagnostic, NOT clean.** CLI `impeccable detect` returned `[]`/exit 0 on `frontend/src`, but also returned `[]` on a control file deliberately stuffed with hardcoded hex, 11px text, unlabeled `<img>`, empty `<button>`. Treat as unavailable.

## Overall Impression

A real engine with no face. The modeling (SRS + EPA power ratings, win-probability bands, Vegas deliberately held to minority weight) is more defensible than most of the competitive set, and completely invisible to a visitor. The front door is an empty h1, the flagship board is an undifferentiated 921-row wall, and on a dark-mode phone the site renders black text on black ground.

**Biggest opportunity:** one weekend of surface work from looking like a real product, because the hard part is done. The gap is presentation, not capability.

## What's Working

1. **`/games` card has genuine craft.** PICK chip, amber UPSET PICK badge, two-tone probability bar, scores at `text-xl font-bold tabular-nums` — real reading order, verdict then evidence. Should be the language the rest of the site inherits.
2. **`MyLeaguesPage` privacy copy is exactly right.** "Nothing leaves your browser — league info (including private-league cookies) is stored locally, not on our server," placed at the moment of hesitation. Trust signal ESPN doesn't give. ESPN cookie walkthrough is the best help text in the app.
3. **Empty states route forward.** `/my-matchups` → "No leagues connected yet — add one from My Leagues" with live link, applied consistently.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Dark mode renders black text on black ground
Verified on `/games` with `prefers-color-scheme: dark`: `body` background computes `rgba(0,0,0,0)` (transparent), `body`/`h1` color computes `rgb(0,0,0)`, `color-scheme` never declared (`normal`). Host paints dark ground, transparent body lets it through, black headings sit on it. `/games` cards survive only via their own `bg-*-50` accents; page chrome (h1, description, week selector, navbar) is black-on-black.
**Why:** peak fantasy traffic is Sunday late morning on phones, large share in dark mode. Broken page, not a slow one.
**Fix:** explicit `background`/`color` on `body` from tokens; declare `color-scheme`; full palette on bare `:root`, redefine tokens under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`. Then ship dark mode properly — Sleeper's dark UI is the category default.
**Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P0] `/redraft` unusable at phone width
375px: `scrollWidth` 418 vs `clientWidth` 375. Two causes — navbar (`News` link at 405px, nav row 419px) clips mid-word with no hamburger; table right edge 384px so `Chg` is cut off. Position filter loses `DST` off the right edge. `RankingsTable` has no responsive treatment: `<table className="w-full text-sm">`.
**Why:** flagship board, on the device and hour it's actually used.
**Fix:** nav behind a sheet below `md`; table collapses to stacked rows below `sm`, dropping `Dome G`/`Chg`; position filter as horizontally scrollable chip row.
**Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] 921 rows, no search/sort/sticky header/tiers
Identical visual weight per row — RB1 looks like the 400th player. Tail ends in negative projections (rank 921, −10.3), reading as "the model is broken," and it's the last impression. Every competitor opens with a search field.
**Fix:** search above table, `sticky top-0` on `<thead>`, sortable Proj/Rank, **positional tier breaks** (fixes chunking + hierarchy + category-specificity in one move), cut render at ~200 with "show more."
**Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Seven dead nav links, one of them the most prominent
`/schedule` (labeled `maybe roster/player`), `/create`, `/join`, `/draft`, `/season`, `/intel`, `News` → unstyled `<p>Page not found</p>`. Three of four dropdowns majority-broken. `News` styled identically to the wordmark = most prominent item in the bar, and it's `to="news"` without leading slash, so it resolves relative to current route (from `/games` → `/games/news`).
**Fix:** delete unbuilt items from `Navbar.tsx`; fix relative `to="news"`; build a real 404 with heading and links to `/redraft` and `/games`.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] No credibility layer on a model-first product
No "updated at" timestamp, no methodology page, no attribution, no legend for `Opp Rk` direction or `80% band`. The differentiator is the model and the site never shows its work — reads as a scraper.
**Fix:** `Rankings updated Sep 15, 9:42 AM ET` under every board header; `/methodology` page explaining SRS + EPA + 20% Vegas weight, linked from footer. Substance already exists in `pipeline.py` docstrings.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (one-handed, phone, Sunday 11:50am — heaviest weight).** Dark-mode iPhone → black screen, invisible headings. Past that, `/redraft` scrolls sideways under his thumb. Board defaults to Rest of Season (wrong for a Sunday lineup); fixing it means a native `<select>` with unlabeled optgroups. Wants his TE; gets a header-less table with no bye week, no injury flag. Opens ESPN.

**Alex (power user).** Wants to sort by projection within WR, then share the view. Neither works — no sortable columns; URL stays `/redraft` so pasted links open ROS/PPR/ALL. Can't compare three flex options: Start/Sit capped at two, `excludeSlug` actively prevents multi-way compare.

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader).** Better than expected; assessments disagreed. Detector measured **zero** empty accessible names across 937 controls on `/redraft`, and a visible focus indicator exists — but it's the **UA default** (`outline: rgb(229,151,0) auto 1px`), nothing authored. `<select>`s expose a name only because the selected option supplies text — "Rest of Season" announces, nothing says what it controls. Position filter is eight bare `<button>`s, no `role="group"`, no group label, no `aria-pressed` — active state conveyed by blue background alone. `NavDropdown` has `role="menu"` but trigger lacks `aria-expanded`/`aria-haspopup`; arrow keys don't work. No skip link.

## Minor Observations

- `RankChangeBadge` renders `NEW` on all 921 rows — `Chg` column is pure noise; hide until a prior week exists.
- `/Dynasty` ships a live placeholder row: "Add dynasty rankings here."
- Footer reads `© 2026` while app renders 2025-season weeks.
- Both teams on every `/games` card show "80%", reading as duplicated data rather than a band label.
- Start/Sit surfaces `Weather —` and `Opp Rank N/A` for ROS entries instead of hiding empty fields.

## Questions to Consider

1. `/games` carries an uncertainty band; Start/Sit, where the season is on the line, gives a bare point estimate and presents a 0.3-point gap with the same confidence as a 9-point gap. Why is the uncertainty on the wrong page?
2. What does this site know that FantasyPros doesn't? Write it in one sentence, put it on the home page, design the home page around proving it.
3. Sunday 11:50am, phone, 90 seconds: what is the one screen that answers "who do I start?" — and why isn't that the front door?
4. The model outputs −10.3 at rank 921. Modeling bug or display bug — and which does a visitor assume?
5. If you deleted the `Proj` column, would anything on `/redraft` still be useful? If not, why is the presentation a 921-row table instead of a tiered shortlist?
